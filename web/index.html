原理

How it works

颜色不是印上去的，
是光穿过来的

The colour isn't printed on.
It's what the light leaves behind.

每一个像素都有自己的一小摞耗材。背光从后面进来，穿过整摞， 每一层减掉自己那份光谱，从正面出来的就是颜色。

Every pixel gets its own little tower of filament. Light enters from behind, passes through the whole stack, each layer subtracts its part of the spectrum, and what leaves the front is a colour.

白色打底把背光打匀；黄吸蓝、品红吸绿、青吸红。层高 0.08 毫米—— 比一根头发还薄，整幅画的全部信息就装在这不到两毫米里。

White diffuses the backlight into an even ground; yellow absorbs blue, magenta absorbs green, cyan absorbs red. At 0.08 mm per layer — thinner than a hair — the entire image lives in under two millimetres of plastic.

难的地方在于三色重叠会变成灰。生成器把这份灰抽出来， 再按每个像素自己的亮度加回去一部分：暗处够沉，亮处干净。换张照片，它自己会重新平衡。

The hard part is that where all three overlap you just get grey. The generator pulls that grey out, then adds part of it back weighted by each pixel's own brightness — shadows stay deep, highlights stay clean. Swap the photo and it rebalances itself.

三步

Three steps

从一张照片到一盏灯

From a photo to a lamp

01

选一张照片Pick a photo

颜色饱和、明暗分明的效果最好。人像、插画、夕阳都很出彩。选好尺寸和外壳颜色。

Saturated colour and clear contrast work best — portraits, illustration, a sunset. Choose a size and a shell colour.

02

看点亮的样子See it lit

浏览器当场算出透光后的效果，不满意就换一张。全程在你的设备上，不联网也能用。

Your browser simulates the backlit result on the spot. Try another if it isn't right — it all runs locally, even offline.

03

下载并打印Download and print

拿到一个排好盘的 3MF：画片和外壳分两盘，AMS 槽位、层高、擦料塔都配好了，导入即可切片。

You get a ready-to-slice 3MF: two plates, AMS slots, layer height and prime tower already set up.
